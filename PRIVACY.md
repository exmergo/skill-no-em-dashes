# Privacy

The no-em-dashes skill collects no data. It runs locally: `SKILL.md` is instructions,
and the bundled checker (`skills/no-em-dashes/scripts/check_em_dashes.py`) reads the
text you give it and prints results in your own terminal. It makes no network requests,
stores nothing, and sends nothing to Exmergo or any third party.

The skill adds no data collection on top of your AI coding assistant. Any text you ask
the assistant to write or edit is handled by that assistant under its provider's own
terms; this skill neither sees nor sends that text anywhere itself.

This skill is open-source software under the MIT license. Its full source, including
the checker, is public, so you can verify everything above for yourself rather than
take it on trust. See the
[repository](https://github.com/exmergo/skill-no-em-dashes) and [LICENSE](LICENSE).
